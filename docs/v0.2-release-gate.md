## Google Ads policy acceptance gate

Before a real pack is called `Google-ready`:
- [ ] current English Google Advertising Policies are checked or local snapshot is fresh;
- [ ] every final banner has an exact-SHA `GOOGLE_POLICY_REVIEWER` result;
- [ ] no misleading system/dialog/pseudo-control/download-install/pseudo-interaction/segmented/transparent-background issue;
- [ ] image/text quality is policy-acceptable;
- [ ] material claims and required qualifiers are verified;
- [ ] real landing page supports exact offer and CTA;
- [ ] destination working/domain/crawlability/geo/accessibility evidence is resolved;
- [ ] advertiser identity and affiliation claims are verified;
- [ ] trademark use is contextually resolved;
- [ ] restricted vertical/certification/targeting states are resolved when applicable;
- [ ] every report and pack aggregation is `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_PASS`;
- [ ] final local status is `GOOGLE_READY_PRECHECK_PASS`;
- [ ] delivery still states final Google review applies and approval is not guaranteed.

Any `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_BLOCKED`, `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_INCOMPLETE`, or `POLICY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` blocks a Google-ready claim.
