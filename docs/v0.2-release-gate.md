## Google Ads policy acceptance gate

Before a real pack is called `Google-ready`:
- [ ] current English Google Advertising Policies are checked or the local snapshot is fresh;
- [ ] every final banner has an exact-SHA `GOOGLE_POLICY_REVIEWER` result;
- [ ] no misleading system/dialog/pseudo-control/download-install/pseudo-interaction/segmented/transparent-background problem;
- [ ] image/text quality is policy-acceptable at actual size;
- [ ] material claims and required qualifiers are verified;
- [ ] landing page supports the exact offer and CTA;
- [ ] destination working/domain/crawlability/geo/accessibility evidence is resolved;
- [ ] advertiser identity and affiliation claims are verified;
- [ ] trademark use is contextually resolved;
- [ ] restricted vertical/certification/targeting states are resolved when applicable;
- [ ] every final report is `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_PASS`;
- [ ] pack policy aggregation is `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_PASS`;
- [ ] final local status is `GOOGLE_READY_PRECHECK_PASS`;
- [ ] delivery still states final Google review applies and approval is not guaranteed.

Any `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_BLOCKED`, `GOOGLE_POLICY_PREFLIGHT_INCOMPLETE`, or `POLICY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` blocks a Google-ready claim.

## Merge rule

Do not merge into `main` merely because deterministic CI is green.

Merge only when:
1. deterministic CI is green on the final head;
2. real Work acceptance gates pass;
3. real-pack Google Ads policy acceptance passes;
4. user's additional style-example integration has been evaluated;
5. independent rigor gates are satisfied, **or** the user explicitly accepts degraded rigor;
6. the user explicitly approves merge.

Until then:
- deterministic engineering: `ACTIVE_HARDENING`;
- real user-acceptance validation: `RETEST_REQUIRED`;
- Google policy readiness: `REAL_PACK_VALIDATION_REQUIRED`;
- delivery/runtime rigor: `NOT_FULLY_VALIDATED`;
- merge: `BLOCKED_BY_RELEASE_GATE`.
