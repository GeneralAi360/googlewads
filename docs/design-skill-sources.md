## Google Advertising Policies — September 7 2026

Canonical policy sources are summarized in:
- `references/google-ads-policy-preflight.md`;
- `config/google-ads-policy-snapshot.json`.

The current English Google Advertising Policies are the enforcement source of truth. The local snapshot is dated, refreshable and explicitly non-guaranteeing.

Policy families relevant to banner delivery include:
- Image ad requirements / Image quality;
- Misrepresentation / Misleading ad design;
- Unreliable claims;
- Unclear relevance / Unavailable offers;
- Destination requirements;
- Trademarks;
- Inappropriate content;
- restricted/sensitive vertical policies/certifications;
- personalized-advertising/targeting restrictions when applicable.

### Misleading-design patterns explicitly screened
- fake system/site warning or error-message mimicry;
- message/dialog/menu/request-notification mimicry;
- non-functional fields/controls/close buttons;
- download/install buttons/icons in image ads;
- misleading arrows/pseudo-interactions;
- transparent background;
- segmented/multiple-ad appearance;
- contextless/disproportionately dominant standalone button.

A normal contextual CTA is not automatically prohibited.

### Claims/destination/trademark
Material claims need verified business evidence and destination support. Advertised offer/price/promotion/CTA must be available/easy to find/use at the landing page before local PASS.

Trademark use is contextual; unresolved reseller/integrator/official-affiliation state returns review-required rather than invented authorization.

## Internal truth model

Reusable design rules are classified as:
1. `PLATFORM REQUIREMENT`;
2. `RESEARCH EVIDENCE`;
3. `PRODUCTION HEURISTIC`;
4. `TEST HYPOTHESIS`.

Observed competitor performance additionally uses A–E evidence tiers.

Google policy uses a separate evidence path because compliance is neither a style preference nor a performance hypothesis.
