## Reconciled architecture findings

1. Meaning needed explicit idea/presentation/emotion/visual-character contracts before style.
2. Lighting needed to derive from meaning/style/AOI rather than decoration.
3. Contemporary style needed controller-level Style Intelligence with capped currentness, three lanes and exact SHA/component provenance.
4. Typography needed real runtime license/file/script/Cyrillic/weight/width/raster verification.
5. REAL-02 required commercial/CTA and brand-identity locks plus real-asset `NEEDS_ASSET` behavior.
6. Google technical validity needed a separate Google Advertising Policy layer.
7. Semantic policy checks must be reviewer-owned and exact-SHA-bound rather than fabricated from deterministic code/OCR.
8. Landing page, material claims, advertiser identity, trademark/affiliation, vertical/certification and targeting evidence are part of Google readiness.
9. `GOOGLE_READY_PRECHECK_PASS` is local risk reduction only and never guarantees actual Google approval.
10. Style Intelligence and Google policy are now explicit top-level phases in canonical `SKILL.md`, not hidden side references.
