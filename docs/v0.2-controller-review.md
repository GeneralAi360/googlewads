8. **Landing page is part of readiness** — working/relevance/offer/CTA/crawlability/accessibility/identity/original-content evidence.
9. **Trademark/affiliation are contextual** — unresolved reseller/integrator/official relationship returns review-required rather than invented authorization.
10. **No approval guarantee** — `GOOGLE_READY_PRECHECK_PASS` remains local risk reduction and explicitly sets approval guarantee false.
11. **Canonical SKILL synchronization** — Style Intelligence, attention/type, pre-render policy risk and final exact-artifact Google policy are now first-class phases directly in `SKILL.md`, not only subordinate references.

## Google policy implementation boundary

Deterministic code proves only what it can actually inspect/verify, including exact file SHA/dimensions/format, PNG transparency, snapshot freshness and structured claim/destination/trademark/vertical evidence consistency.

Semantic policy review is bound to exact final artifact SHA and covers misleading design, image/text quality, pseudo-controls, download/install UI, misleading arrows/interactions, segmented/multi-ad appearance, inappropriate content and deceptive manipulation.

Policy is separate from performance. No policy status predicts CTR/CVR or actual Google moderation outcome.
